I am a computational mathematics researcher. My research interests focus on numerical analysis and scientific computing for geometric partial differential equations.

I obtained my PhD degree from Yau Mathematical Science Center, Tsinghua University in 2025, under the supervision of Prof. [Chunmei Su](http://www.chunmei-su.cn/). After this, I was a postdoctoral research assistant at Department of Mathematics, University of Edinburgh and Mathematical Institute, University of Oxford, mentored by Prof. [Kaibo Hu](https://kaibohu.github.io/).

I am current a postdoctoral research assistant at Department of Applied Mathematics, the Hong Kong Polytechnic University, mentored by Prof. [Buyang Li](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/ama/profile/byli/).

I grew up in Ningbo and Hangzhou, China, and have since lived in Jinan, Beijing, Regensburg, Edinburgh, Oxford and Hong Kong.
